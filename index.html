        Partner with IIT Guwahati for PhD recruitment

        The Centre for Career Development is your single window to PhD talent across all departments. We work with companies and institutions on:

        • PhD recruitment. Full-time roles, research internships and the 2-PhD Pilot: two research interns or two PhD hires in a season.
        • MoUs for PhD hiring. A long-term agreement for regular PhD intake, internships and joint research with IIT Guwahati.

        Write to placement@iitg.ac.in or visit www.iitg.ac.in/ccd.
